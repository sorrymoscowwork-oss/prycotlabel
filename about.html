About PRYCOT.

PRYCOT. / ABOUTABOUT

Independent music distribution built around crypto, human communication and independent infrastructure.

PRYCOT. is a new kind of music distributor built for artists who want to work independently from traditional banking systems.

01

Independent Payments

We are building a distribution service where artists can pay for releases in cryptocurrency and receive their royalties in cryptocurrency. No bank account or traditional bank card is required.

Our goal is to create an independent financial flow for music — international, direct and accessible regardless of where an artist lives or which banking system they use.

02

More Than Distribution

We believe music distribution should be about more than simply sending a release to streaming platforms. PRYCOT. is also developing as an independent label working with artists directly.

Every PRYCOT. client has the opportunity to be considered for our label and receive additional support in developing and promoting their music.

When we take an artist under our curation, we can work with their release, develop a promotional strategy and help distribute their music across the internet through targeted advertising and other promotional tools.

03

Human Communication

Human communication is at the centre of PRYCOT.

We currently work with our clients personally. Every release, payment, distribution detail and individual request can be discussed directly with a member of our team — not through bots, automated support or algorithms.

As the number of artists grows, part of this process may eventually move online, but personal communication will remain an important part of how we work.

04

Our Team

Our team brings together marketers, music strategists, music video directors, musicians, mixing engineers and other music professionals.

This allows us to work with an artist not only at the distribution stage, but across different parts of the release and promotion process.

05

Specialized Services

We also provide specialized creative and music services paid for in cryptocurrency, depending on the needs of the artist.

06

Worldwide

PRYCOT. is being built as a label and distribution platform for artists worldwide.

Your country, bank, card or access to traditional financial services should not determine whether you can release your music.

RELEASE WORLDWIDE. PAY IN CRYPTO.

07

PRYCOTLINK — Free Release Pages

PRYCOTLINK is a free service that lets artists create public pages for their music releases and share everything through a single link. Each page can bring together release information, cover artwork, streaming links, social profiles, videos, and a short description — making it easier for listeners to find the music and follow the artist.

Artists can create multiple pages and update them from their PRYCOT. account whenever details or links change.
