PRYCOT. / DASHBOARD GUIDE

Your PRYCOT. Dashboard.

The Dashboard is your personal release area. Sign in with Telegram to access your account, submit releases through the official PRYCOT. Telegram bot, and see the releases connected to your Telegram account. You do not upload audio or artwork to the website.

01

Create an account

Go to Account and create your PRYCOT. account, or sign in if you already have one. Your account is where your release information will be shown.

02

Sign in with Telegram

Open Account and sign in with Telegram. Your Telegram account identifies your PRYCOT. account and connects the releases you submit through the release bot.

There is no separate CONNECT TELEGRAM step. Telegram is the account sign-in and the identity used to associate your releases with your Dashboard.

03

Submit your release through Telegram

When Telegram is connected, submit your release through the PRYCOT. bot. The bot collects the release information and receives the audio and artwork.

You do not need to upload large audio files through the website. The website is used for your account and Dashboard; Telegram is used for submitting the release materials.

04

Your release appears in the Dashboard

After you complete the release request in the bot and press CONFIRM, the release is added to your Dashboard and sent to the PRYCOT. admin.

You can see the artist name, release title, release type, release date and current status of each release. Your status is updated when the PRYCOT. admin changes it in the Admin Dashboard.

05

Check your releases whenever you need

Return to your account at any time to see all releases connected to your Telegram account and their current statuses. You do not need to create a new account for each release.

In short: sign in with Telegram → submit your release through the PRYCOT. bot → confirm the request → your release appears in the Dashboard → follow its status.
