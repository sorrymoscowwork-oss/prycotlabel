PRYCOT. / HOW TO RELEASEHOW TO RELEASE

Release your music. Worldwide. In a few simple steps.

The PRYCOT. release process starts in your account and is completed through the official Telegram release bot. Sign in with Telegram, prepare your release, then submit the request through the bot.

01

Prepare the release

Before submitting, make sure your final audio, artwork and release information are ready. Check every title, artist name, date and other metadata carefully.

02

Open the release request

Sign in to your PRYCOT. account with Telegram and use SUBMIT A RELEASE. The button opens the official PRYCOT. Telegram release bot and starts the release request.

03

Complete the request in Telegram

The bot will collect the artist name, release title, release type, genre, release date, explicit-content status, number of tracks, track titles, audio files and artwork. Enter everything exactly as it should appear on the final release. The bot accepts WAV audio and JPG/PNG artwork.

04

Review before confirmation

Before confirming the request, check the summary shown by the bot. The information submitted and confirmed there becomes the basis of your release request. For the detailed bot procedure, see the Telegram Bot Guide.

05

Submit & pay

After you press CONFIRM, the request is sent to the PRYCOT. admin and the release is added to your Dashboard. The applicable distribution fee is paid in cryptocurrency before distribution begins.

06

Moderation & distribution

PRYCOT. reviews the release package and, when accepted, submits the release to the available digital distribution system. Platform processing and publication times may vary. You can follow the current release status in your Dashboard.

07

Receive your royalties

Royalties are calculated from distributor reporting. Payments are processed every three months. Royalties are paid in stablecoins: USDT TRC20 or USDT TON. The current royalty terms are described in Release Terms.

Important. The PRYCOT. Telegram bot is the official release submission channel. Enter all information carefully and without errors. Your release request is prepared from the information you enter and confirm in the bot.
