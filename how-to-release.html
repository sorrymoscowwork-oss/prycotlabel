PRYCOT. / HOW TO RELEASE

Release your music. Worldwide. In a few simple steps.

Everything you need to prepare and submit a release to PRYCOT. for digital distribution.

01

Prepare your music

Submit the final master in WAV or FLAC, 16 bit / 44.1 kHz or higher. The audio should be free of clipping, technical defects and unwanted noise.

02

Prepare your artwork

Artwork should be JPG or PNG, 3000 × 3000 px, RGB. Make sure you have permission to use every third-party image, logo or other material.

03

Prepare your metadata

Provide the artist name, release title, track titles, release type, genre, language, release date and all required author, performer and featuring information.

04

Contact PRYCOT.

Send your release details to a PRYCOT. manager via Telegram. We discuss the release directly with you and check that everything is ready for distribution.

05

Pay in crypto

Choose the release type and pay the distribution fee in cryptocurrency before distribution begins.

06

Moderation & distribution

We review the release package and submit the approved release to the available digital platforms. Platform processing times may vary.

07

Receive your royalties

Royalties are calculated from platform reporting. The artist or rights holder receives 90%, while PRYCOT. retains 10%. Royalties are paid in cryptocurrency once every three months.
