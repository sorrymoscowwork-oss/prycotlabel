PRYCOT. / HOW TO RELEASE

Release your music. Worldwide. In a few simple steps.

The PRYCOT. release process is handled through your account and the official PRYCOT. Telegram release bot. Prepare your release first, then submit the request through the bot.

01

Prepare the release

Before submitting, make sure your final audio, artwork and release information are ready. Check every title, artist name, date and other metadata carefully.

02

Open the release request

Sign in to your PRYCOT. account and use SUBMIT A RELEASE. The button opens the official PRYCOT. Telegram bot, which is used to collect the release request.

03

Complete the request in Telegram

The bot will guide you through the required release information and ask you to send the audio files. Enter everything exactly as it should appear on the final release. Do not leave accidental spelling errors, incorrect dates or other inaccuracies.

04

Review before confirmation

Before confirming the request, check the summary shown by the bot. The information submitted and confirmed there becomes the basis of your release request. For the detailed bot procedure, see the Telegram Bot Guide.

05

Submit & pay

After the request is submitted, PRYCOT. processes the release according to the submitted information. The applicable distribution fee is paid in cryptocurrency before distribution begins.

06

Moderation & distribution

PRYCOT. reviews the release package and submits the approved release to the available digital platforms. Platform processing and publication times may vary.

07

Receive your royalties

Royalties are calculated from distributor reporting. Payments are processed every three months. Royalties are paid in stablecoins: USDT TRC20 or USDT TON.

Important. The PRYCOT. Telegram bot is the official release submission channel. Enter all information carefully and without errors. Your release request is prepared from the information you enter and confirm in the bot.
