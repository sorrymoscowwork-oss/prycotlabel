Label Services.

Additional services for artists working with PRYCOT. as an independent label. Each service is discussed directly with our team and can be paid for in cryptocurrency.

01

Producer Supervision & Control

PRYCOT. works with artists as a producer and creative partner throughout the development of their music project. The producer can work with the artist personally in the Middle East or remotely with artists anywhere in the world.

We oversee the project from the recording stage through to its final presentation. This includes working directly with the artist on the music, advising on creative decisions, developing the artist’s image and positioning, and defining the overall artistic direction of the project.

The producer acts as the creative lead of the project — helping the artist understand what needs to be changed, developed or refined to bring the music to a higher professional level. This can include guidance on recording, arrangements, performances, music videos, visual identity and live performances.

We can also work directly with directors, musicians, engineers, managers and other members of the artist’s team, coordinating the creative direction of the project as a whole.

Full album production and creative supervision — from $3,000.
