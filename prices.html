Main rule

The artist receives 90% of reported royalties.

PRYCOT. retains 10% of the total royalties reported for each period as its service fee. This is one deduction from royalties, not an additional fee on top of it. Payments are made in cryptocurrency every 3 months.

Stablecoins

USDT (TON, TRC20) and USDC (Arbitrum) are accepted at their nominal value of $1 USD per token.

Other cryptocurrencies

Bitcoin, Ethereum and other supported coins are accepted at the current market rate at the time of payment.

Royalty payments

Royalties are paid in stablecoins.

Royalty payments are made exclusively in USDT on TRC20 or USDT on TON, based on the reporting metrics received from our distributors.
Payments are processed every 3 months.
