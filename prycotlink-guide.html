PRYCOTLINK Guide

PRYCOT. / PRYCOTLINK GUIDE

Your release page. Your links. One place.

PRYCOTLINK is a public page for an artist or music release. It brings release information, streaming links, social profiles and videos together in one shareable URL.

01

What is PRYCOTLINK?

It is a simple public landing page that helps listeners find your release and your official links in one place. Share the page in social media, messages, artist profiles or promotional materials.

PRYCOTLINK is a promotional page, not a music distributor. It does not deliver audio to streaming platforms or replace the PRYCOT release submission process.

02

Sign in to your PRYCOT account

Open Account and sign in with Telegram. Your account is the place where you create and manage your public PRYCOTLINK pages.

03

Create a page from your account

Once signed in, find the PRYCOTLINK section in your account and click CREATE PRYCOTLINK →. This opens the page editor. Complete the release information, add a cover, and include your streaming, social and video links as needed.

Choose a unique page address. When you submit the form successfully, your public page is created and can be opened through its URL.

04

Manage your pages in one place

Your created pages appear in the PRYCOTLINK section of your account. Open a page to view it, or use its edit option to update the information and save your changes.

05

Create more than one release page

You can create multiple PRYCOTLINK pages from the same account. Each page has its own unique URL and appears in your account, so you can manage pages for different releases without creating a separate account for each one.

In short: sign in to Account with Telegram → click CREATE PRYCOTLINK → complete the form → share your public page → return to Account whenever you need to edit it.

PRYCOTLINK is free. Create as many pages as you need and use every feature without restrictions. If the service has been useful to you, consider supporting the project.
