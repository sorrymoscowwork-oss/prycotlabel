RELEASE TERMS

Release Terms

These terms define the requirements for submitting music to PRYCOT. for digital distribution. Please make sure your materials and metadata meet the requirements below before confirming your release request.

00

Payment

Release fees are paid in cryptocurrency according to the current PRYCOT. pricing. Payment must be completed before distribution processing begins.

01

Release Types

  • Single — 1 track
  • Maxi Single — up to 3 tracks
  • EP — up to 6 tracks
  • Album — up to 20 tracks
02

Audio

Audio files must be WAV. The bot rejects files that do not meet the required format.

03

Artwork

Artwork must be exactly 3000 × 3000 pixels. The bot rejects artwork that does not meet this requirement.

04

Metadata

Provide accurate artist name, release title, release type, genre, release date, and explicit-content information.

05

Rights

You must have the necessary rights and permissions for all submitted audio, artwork, names, and other materials.

06

Moderation

PRYCOT. reviews submitted materials before distribution. If an uploaded file is incorrect or the submission contains an inconsistency, PRYCOT. may contact the user privately through Telegram to clarify or request the correct material.

07

Distribution

Approved releases are submitted for digital distribution to supported platforms, including international and Russian services where available.

09

Analytics

Analytics are provided every 3 months together with royalty payouts.

10

Royalties

Royalties are paid in cryptocurrency.

11

Takedown & Updates

Requests for takedowns or metadata and release updates are handled through the PRYCOT. release process.

12

Additional Services

Additional services may include producer supervision and consulting related to cryptocurrency payments.
