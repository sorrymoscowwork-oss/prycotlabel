RELEASE TERMS

Release Terms

These terms define the requirements for submitting music to PRYCOT. for digital distribution. Please make sure your materials and metadata meet the requirements below before confirming your release request.

00

Payment

Release fees are paid in cryptocurrency according to the current PRYCOT. pricing. Payment must be completed before distribution processing begins.

01

Release Types

  • Single — 1 track
  • Maxi Single — up to 3 tracks
  • EP — up to 6 tracks
  • Album — up to 20 tracks
02

Audio

Audio must be submitted as WAV. Bitrate, bit depth, sample rate and file size are not separate requirements of the PRYCOT. release bot.

Each track must be submitted as its own audio file.

03

Artwork

Artwork must be submitted as JPG or PNG, 3000 × 3000 px. This is a mandatory requirement. The PRYCOT. release bot rejects artwork that does not meet this size requirement.

04

Metadata

Artist name and release title may be written in any language. Genre is entered manually. Release date must follow the format requested by the bot. Explicit content is selected as Yes or No.

05

Rights

You must own or control the rights necessary to distribute all submitted recordings, compositions, artwork and other materials. Do not submit material that infringes third-party rights.

06

Moderation

PRYCOT. reviews submitted release information and materials before distribution. If an uploaded file is incorrect or the submission contains an inconsistency, PRYCOT. may contact the user privately through Telegram to clarify or request the correct material.

07

Distribution

Approved releases are submitted to the available digital distribution system. Publication timing and availability are subject to the processing of external digital platforms and services.

09

Analytics

Release analytics and royalty information are provided based on distributor reporting. Analytics are reviewed every three months together with royalty reporting.

10

Royalties

Royalties are paid every three months in cryptocurrency according to the applicable PRYCOT. royalty terms. Payments are made in supported stablecoins, including USDT TRC20 or USDT TON where applicable.

11

Takedown & Updates

After submission, metadata or release changes and takedown requests may be requested through PRYCOT. The processing time for changes or removals may depend on the distribution system and external platforms.

12

Additional Services

PRYCOT. may provide additional services such as producer supervision, release consulting and crypto-related consultation where agreed separately.
